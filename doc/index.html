Large File Log Viewer

Built for troubleshooting

Millions of lines.
Faster answers.

Open log files produced by your application's logger. Search across files, follow new entries, bookmark errors and export matching lines in one native workspace.

01

Browse large files with ease

Index lines in the background, decode visible content on demand, and keep memory use bounded with virtualized tables.

02

Search across files

Combine keywords, case matching and byte-based regex. Search the current tab, multiple open tabs, or a directory.

errortimeoutretry
03

Keep up with live logs

Follow the latest lines. Append-only updates extend the index; truncation or replacement triggers a safe rebuild.

04

Keep track of every clue

Use bookmarks, ten color labels, severity colors and match highlights to preserve the trail of your investigation.

05

Resume your workspace

Save tab order, queries, bookmarks, reading position and display preferences. Continue where you left off.

06

Take your results with you

Stream current or cross-file results to disk, grouped by file or stably merged by log timestamp.

service.logworker.log→results.log
